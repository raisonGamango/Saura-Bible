1. Introduction

Welcome to Saura Bible, a Bible and Christian resource application developed by Saura Site.

Saura Bible provides Bible content in the Soura/Saura language together with Christian and devotional resources such as Sunday programme references, songs, videos, news resources, dictionary content and other faith-related materials.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Saura Bible mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

Saura Bible does not require users to create an account to use the core application and does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

✓ Name
✓ Email address
✓ Phone number
✓ Home address
✓ Passwords
✓ Contacts
✓ Photos
✓ Financial information

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

2.2 Technical Information

The app may communicate with online services to provide certain features and content. Third-party services used by the app may process technical information according to their own policies.

Google Play Data Safety: The current Google Play listing for Saura Bible states that no data is shared with third parties and no data is collected by the developer. These declarations should be kept consistent with the actual SDKs and services included in the released app.

3. Advertising

The current Google Play listing indicates that Saura Bible contains ads.

Advertising providers may use technologies such as advertising identifiers or similar technical information to deliver, measure, personalize, or improve advertisements, depending on the provider, the user's region, device settings, and applicable requirements.

Saura Site does not sell users' personal information. Third-party advertising providers are responsible for their own processing practices and privacy policies.

If the advertising SDK or any other third-party SDK in a future version collects or shares data, the Google Play Data Safety declaration and this Privacy Policy should be updated accordingly.

4. How Information Is Used

We do not intentionally use personal information to:

  • Sell or rent personal information.
  • Create personal user profiles for marketing.
  • Send unsolicited promotional messages.
  • Maintain a personal user-account database.

Where third-party services process technical information, they may use it for purposes such as providing advertisements, maintaining functionality, security, analytics, fraud prevention, or preventing abuse, subject to their own policies.

5. Bible Content and Online Resources

Some Saura Bible features or resources may require an internet connection. When you access online content, your device may communicate with the relevant online service.

External websites, videos, online resources, news services, and other third-party content are controlled by their respective providers and may have separate privacy policies.

6. Third-Party Services

The app may use or provide access to third-party services, websites, advertisements, videos, or online content, including services associated with Google Play and advertising providers.

These third parties may collect or process information under their own privacy policies. Saura Site does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party providers.

7. Children's Privacy

Saura Bible contains Bible, Christian, educational, devotional, and language-related material and is intended for a general audience.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through the application. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate the matter.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application and to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

Because Saura Bible does not maintain a user account database or intentionally collect personal information for our own user database, there is generally no personal user database maintained by us through the app.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Saura Bible does not intentionally maintain a personal user-account database. Therefore, there is generally no personal account data stored by us that requires an account-deletion request.

If you believe Saura Site may have received personal information from you and you would like it reviewed or deleted, contact us using the information below.
